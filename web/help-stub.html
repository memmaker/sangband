Keys to remember

A stand-in until the full guide is built (web/make-help.py). Press ? in the game for its own command list and help files.

Enter
Command menu: every command, grouped
H
Auto-explore (roguelike keys: O)
< >
Walk to the nearest known staircase; press again to take it
i e
Inventory / equipment with a cursor and item menus
?
The game's help
Ctrl+S
Save (the page also saves every two minutes and when you leave the tab)
Saves
Kept in this browser (IndexedDB). A reload continues your newest living character. File ▾ exports all saves as one file and imports it again.
Windows
Drag the gutters, drag a title bar to move a window, A−/A+ on a title bar sizes its text (on the Map: the zoom). Windows ▾ shows or hides windows.
